verifier, all web-sourced). The site had 17 unique callers all-time and 7 in the trailing 28 days

## Verify before signing (nothing here is public-searchable by bot; do it by hand)

Hawaii has two separate credentials:

1. **DCCA Pest Control Board PCO license** (HRS 460J), issued by **branch**: Branch 1 = fumigation,
   Branch 2 = general pest, Branch 3 = termite. To do the site's five services in-house the anchor
   needs 2 + 3, plus 1 to tent themselves. **Ask every finalist: "which branches is your PCO
   licensed for?"** Look it up at [mypvl.dcca.hawaii.gov public license search](https://mypvl.dcca.hawaii.gov/public-license-search/)
   by PCO number (PCO1267 Mason, PCO-1690 Pacific, PCO1610 Lanaki, PCO1473 Elite).
2. **HDOA Pesticides Branch applicator certification**, by category (structural = 7a-7e, 10).
   Public PDF roster of certified applicators by company and town at
   [dab.hawaii.gov certified applicators](https://dab.hawaii.gov/pi/files/2025/02/Certified-Applicators-02.07.2025.pdf).
   HDOA Pesticides Branch: (808) 973-9402.

Also: complaint history at [DCCA RICO search](https://web2.dcca.hawaii.gov/rico/#/search), and
request a certificate of insurance directly (not public).
